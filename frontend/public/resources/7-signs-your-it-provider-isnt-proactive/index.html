7 Signs Your IT Provider Isn't Proactive

If your IT provider only calls when something breaks, these seven telling signs confirm the problem is bigger than you think.

Share

"Proactive IT support" is one of the most common phrases in MSP marketing - and one of the least consistently delivered. These seven signs reveal whether your provider is actually living up to that promise.

1. You Only Hear From Them When Something Breaks

A genuinely proactive provider reaches out with findings, recommendations, and check-ins - not just when a ticket needs resolving.

2. You've Never Seen Monitoring Data or Reports

If your provider can't show you actual data about your network's health and security posture, it's unclear what, if anything, they're actively monitoring.

3. The Same Issues Keep Recurring

Recurring problems suggest root causes are being patched over repeatedly rather than actually resolved.

4. No Regular Strategic or Business Reviews

Proactive providers schedule recurring reviews to discuss performance, risk, and upcoming needs - not just ad-hoc conversations when you happen to reach out.

5. They Can't Tell You What They Prevented

A strong provider can point to specific threats detected and stopped before causing damage. If they can't, it raises real questions about what active monitoring is actually happening.

6. Patch and Update Status Is a Mystery

If you don't know (and they can't easily tell you) your current patch compliance status, proactive management isn't really happening.

7. Your Technology Roadmap Doesn't Exist

Proactive IT includes forward-looking planning, not just reactive maintenance. No roadmap suggests a purely reactive relationship.
