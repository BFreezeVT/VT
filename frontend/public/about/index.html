Our Story

From Break-Fix to a Proactive Partner

Veracity Technologies began in the 1980s as Tech-Com, a break-fix IT company focused on solving technology problems quickly and reliably. As technology advanced, we recognized that businesses needed more than reactive support. They needed proactive management that prevents problems before they disrupt operations.

Founded in 1989 and based in Minnetonka, Minnesota, we evolved into Veracity Technologies, a SOC 2 certified Managed Service Provider dedicated to keeping clients secure, compliant, and productive through modern, tailored IT solutions and long-term partnerships. We chose the name Veracity because technology-dependent businesses require precision, accuracy, and truth from their IT partner in order to get high-performing technology, access, integration, and return on investment.

Today, we serve small and midsize businesses across the Twin Cities metro and beyond, with deep expertise in financial services, construction, manufacturing, and other high-compliance industries. Our long-term partnerships and 98% retention rate reflect a simple commitment: keep technology reliable, secure, and ready for growth, so our clients can focus on their business.

What Makes Veracity Different from Other MSPs

Choosing an IT partner is a decision about trust. Here is what Twin Cities businesses get when they choose Veracity.

SOC 2 Certified Security

Veracity is a SOC 2 certified Managed Service Provider, which means our security controls are independently audited. Your data, your clients, and your compliance obligations are protected by enterprise-grade controls, not promises.

Built for High-Compliance Industries

We specialize in financial services, construction, manufacturing, and other high-compliance industries in the Minneapolis-St. Paul area. We understand regulatory requirements, audit preparation, and the cost of getting compliance wrong.

Fast Response, 24/7 Monitoring

When you call our dedicated helpdesk, we guarantee service within 7 minutes or less. Around-the-clock monitoring identifies and resolves issues before they disrupt your operations, so small problems never become downtime.

Long-Term Partnerships, Not Transactions

Our 98% client retention rate is the result of long-term relationships. We work closely with each client, learn their environment, and invest in their success. Many of our clients have partnered with us for a decade or more.

Expert Training and Support

We offer comprehensive training and continuous support to keep your team productive. Our technicians understand your systems and your people, and they explain solutions in plain language, not jargon.

Proven Expertise, Recognized Nationally

With more than 35 years in business, Veracity has been named an MSP 501 Award recipient for eight consecutive years, a CRN Tech Elite 250 and CRN Security 100 provider, and a Channel Futures Top 501 Managed Services Provider.
